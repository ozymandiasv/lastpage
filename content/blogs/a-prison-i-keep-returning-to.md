Yesterday, we had a party with a few friends. We talked, laughed, drank and had a genuinely good time, but somehow, as if following a ritual nobody remembers agreeing to, the conversation eventually found its way to my past, to the woman I loved and to everything that broke me as a person. They would bring her up, revisit those memories and perhaps unknowingly reopen something I have spent years trying to leave behind. And every single time, it hurts exactly as it did the first time. I don’t know whether they realise what happens to me afterwards, but the conversation ends, everyone moves on, and I am left carrying something they have already forgotten. I had a wonderful day, drank until I was barely conscious, laughed with people I genuinely enjoy being around, and yet the pain followed me home. It stays for another couple of days, quietly occupying every corner of my mind. Sometimes I wonder whether I will ever get out of this. Am I consciously choosing to remain a prisoner in a prison whose doors might have been open for years? Have I become so accustomed to punishing myself that letting go feels almost like betraying something I once held dear? Or perhaps there is a part of me that still believes suffering is the only way to honour what I lost. I honestly don’t know. It has been such a long time, and yet here I am, after everyone has gone to sleep, writing in the darkness of the night with a cigarette in my hand, the smell of whiskey lingering around me and my head still spinning. Perhaps I am trying to drown the memories, perhaps I am trying to argue with them, or perhaps I simply don’t know what else to do when they return. I sometimes wish I could kill my regrets, not revisit them every time somebody mentions her, not spend another night negotiating with memories that have already taken enough from me. Whatever happiness that love once gave me, the pain it has left behind feels considerably greater now. And that is perhaps the cruelest part of it all: I don’t even know whether I miss the person anymore, or whether I am simply unable to stop missing the person I used to be before everything happened. I want to move on, I really do. But some nights, I wonder whether I have forgotten how.
