I honestly don’t know what Messi has left to surprise us with. At 39, when most footballers have already walked away from the game, he is still finding ways to make us sit up and wonder how he continues to do this. Yesterday’s free kick was one of those moments. From an angle where most players would probably look for a cross, Messi saw the goal, took the risk and curled the ball beautifully into the far corner. It was the kind of free kick that looks almost casual when he does it, which somehow makes it even more ridiculous.

What I love about watching Messi at this stage of his career is that he doesn’t need to dominate an entire match anymore to remind everyone who he is. A few touches, one pass, a piece of movement or a free kick can suddenly produce something that nobody else on the pitch seemed capable of imagining. There is something almost funny about it now. Every year we think we have reached the point where age must finally have taken something away from him, and then he does something like this and makes that assumption look foolish.

Inter Miami eventually lost the match 2–1 to Columbus, but the result almost feels secondary when you have watched a moment like that. Football is full of players who were brilliant for a few years and then slowly disappeared from the highest level. Messi somehow keeps finding these little reminders that the imagination that made him extraordinary in the first place hasn’t disappeared. The little man is 39, and somehow he is still surprising us.
