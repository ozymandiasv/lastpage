Through the hardest years of my life, the single beacon of pure joy was watching Messi lift the World Cup. To realize he has worn that iconic sky-blue and white jersey for the very last time leaves a heavy, hollow quiet. Witnessing his career has been the greatest privilege of my lifetime. No other human has ever conjured such effortless magic on grass; he didn’t just play the game, he defined it for me. Farewell, GOAT.
