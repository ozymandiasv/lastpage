The true culprit is the inherent human tendency to acquire power, and religion happens to be one of the easiest tools for it. Reducing religion to nothing but institutionalization and indoctrination is a privileged perspective. Many people deeply believe in karma and divine wrath. While this may seem contradictory, it is reality: religion is stupid to the wise, necessary for maintaining order among the weak, and a tool of manipulation for the exploiters.
