In 2014, a monthly per capita spending below ₹1,407 defined urban extreme poverty. Today, standard metrics artificially anchor survival between ₹1,200 and ₹1,800. Yet, given an 80% cumulative increase in inflation, a realistic 2026 threshold must exceed ₹2,500. This disparity shows how easily baseline calculations can be manipulated for political or statistical convenience. Let's forget about PCI and CPI.
