Congress Prime Minister Manmohan Singh was once greeted with black flags at JNU, but Manmohan Singh upheld the student's right to protest and asked not to take any action on their democratic rights. Now India has a group of unaccountable politicians who are suppressing the voice of protesters. Congress has done it during the Emergency, but later they never suppressed voices, especially during the Lokpal Protests, because they feared the media and feared the public. Probably no politicians in the world ever would have thought they could evade media except one.
