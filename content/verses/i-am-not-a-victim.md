title: "I Am Not a Victim"
---

I am someone who does not speak for himself,
and perhaps that is why my emotions are always questioned.
I am asked to explain myself,
to justify why I was not this or that
at that particular moment,
as though every silence needs an explanation.

I am summoned, blamed,
and then asked to defend myself against speculations.
Before I can even find the words,
the blame arrives again,
without prelude, without warning.
And I am still someone who does not speak for himself.
So I take it.
I get angry.
I rage.
And eventually, I forget.

My blood has always found reasons to blame me
for mistakes I made once,
mistakes that have long since passed,
mistakes that have nothing to do
with the consequences of today.
Yet somehow, the past is always brought back
to explain a present it never caused.

Even God, apparently, cannot forgive me
for the prayers I did not say.

But I am not a victim.

I am simply someone who lost his way years ago
and never quite found the road back.
Someone who does not know how to speak to people anymore,
but still knows how to write.

So I write.

Not because I want anyone to pity me.
Not because I am asking to be understood.
And certainly not because I am ranting.

I am just being me,
alone,
trying to make sense of the noise
without having to explain myself
to anyone.
