I ask no one to be productive.
A word they brought into existence because slavery sounded too much like subjugation.
But apparently, being productive is now the measure of a growing life.
Ambition is the new reward, and salaries are the new crumbs.

I will not ask you not to waste your time.
Waste it as much as you want.
Waste an entire afternoon doing nothing.
Spend a night staring at the ceiling.
Watch a stupid film, read a book you will never finish, sit by the window and watch the rain.
But waste the time you own, not the time you have leased from giant companies selling you content.

Your ASMR is smiling at a cat being cut into sand.
Your dopamine wants another colour, another face, another thing to look at.
So you scroll through thumbnails upon thumbnails without even opening them.
You are happy just looking.
You don’t even need to know what lies behind them.
You were once a slave at work.
Now you are a slave on your bed.

You wake up to your phone’s notifications.
You reach for it before you have even gathered your thoughts.
A silent night feels strange.
An empty screen with no notification in the morning feels unbearable.
You cannot fall asleep without scrolling through reels until your phone slips from your hand and lands on your nose.

You are building your own prison, brick by brick, as though you hate yourself.
A kind of torture you would not wish upon your enemy.
And the strange thing is that nobody has to force you into it anymore.
You carry the prison in your pocket.
You take it to bed.
You reach for it the moment you wake.

So waste your time.
Waste it freely.
But make sure it is yours.
Own your entertainment, your nights, your sleep.
Own the hours that serve no purpose and lead nowhere.
Own the silence without feeling the need to fill it.
Own the boredom without reaching for a screen to rescue you from it.

Because you do not have to turn every moment into something useful.
You do not have to make your rest productive.
You are allowed to do nothing, provided that the nothing belongs to you.

The giant companies already have enough of your time.
They have your attention, your habits, your impulses, and the hours you cannot remember spending.
They sell you the content, measure your attention, and keep you watching.

At least own the time you waste, because the rest of your life is already being sold.
