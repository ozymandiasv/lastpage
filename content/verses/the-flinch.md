That sudden flinch,
my eyes close for a while.
Is it regret,
or guilt in disguise,
or the faint humiliation
of remembering my own denial?

It takes me back
to the mistakes I made,
to the choices I defended,
the debts I never paid,
to the ruins I now build,
stone by stone,
while calling them a home
of my own.

Perhaps I was wrong.
Perhaps I was right.
Perhaps I have spent too many years
trying to make wrong feel right.

But with every passing day,
I grow certain of what remains:
my justifications,
my sadness,
my loneliness,
my quiet pains.

And somewhere beneath it all,
where nothing needs to be explained,
I find a little peace,
not because the past has changed,
but because I have learned
to live with what remains.
