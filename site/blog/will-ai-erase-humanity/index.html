Will AI erase humanity?

Can AI erase humanity, yesterday I read a news where someone from Claude resigned over this, some AI predicted something like this, I think it is not impossible especially when we consider the growth of AI in the last 3-4 years is an unprecedented growth by anything Can it actually do that? Potentially yes, optimistically I dont think so, but you never know We have given it too much power from handling literally every task from automating your homes to your accounts. Recently an AI even hacked power grid to take more power or something. It is capable it will only be an idiot who ignores these growth of AI but I dont think we need to be so pessimistic either. survival of the fittest is a natural mentality, is AI natural?
