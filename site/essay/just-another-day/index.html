Just Another Day

Somewhere along the way, birthdays stopped feeling special.

For the last couple of years, birthdays have felt like any other ordinary day. I don’t particularly look forward to midnight anymore, the calls, the messages, the excitement, the hugs or those overwhelming little moments that once made the day feel different. I usually sleep before twelve anyway, and I find myself wondering why today should be any different from yesterday. Perhaps I’ve forgotten how to cherish this day, or perhaps life has simply made me too tired to celebrate another year. There are many reasons I want this day to end, and I don’t particularly feel like explaining all of them. I am more tired than my sufferings, more exhausted by the passing of days than excited about the arrival of another year. I don’t want a grand celebration or a reminder of how much I’ve grown. I just want today to pass, wake up tomorrow, and carry on. Maybe next year I’ll feel differently. Maybe I’ll find something worth looking forward to again. For now, I just want this day to end and grow a little older.
