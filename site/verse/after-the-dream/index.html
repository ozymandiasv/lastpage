After the Dream

19 Sept 2026

Is it my dreams that simply wake me,
Or are my dreams, somehow, being returned?
Do I awake in misery and vain,
While I alone regret the whole of it?
My thoughts are wounded; so is something deep,
A quiet ache that I can never name.
But where, exactly, does the pain lie?

Perhaps it lies within the dream itself,
Where you have somehow taken part in mine.
And if you are a part of this strange dream,
Do you, too, dream the dream that I have dreamed?
Do you behold another side of it,
A world in which my place is not the same?
Or have you long forgotten I exist?

Perhaps tomorrow comes without a trace,
Another day disguised as intuition.
And I remain to question what was real,
What I imagined, and what you once knew.
Was I alone inside that fragile world,
Or did we meet within the same strange dream?

Do I cry to God, whom I know not,
Whom I have never truly thought exists?
Perhaps I ask for only this of Him:
Not that the dream should finally come true,
But that I might not wake to dream it twice.

For waking is not always an escape.
Sometimes the dream survives the morning light,
And follows quietly where I cannot.
Perhaps the only time I invoke God
Is to make me not see such dreams again,
Rather than simply wake me from them.
